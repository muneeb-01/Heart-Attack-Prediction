Cardiovascular Risk Predictors

The prediction engine evaluates 11 critical physiological and clinical attributes shown to correlate directly with coronary artery blockages.

Age & Sex

Fundamental biological baselines key to age-related arterial stiffness and hormonal indicators.

Resting Blood Pressure

Systolic pressure (mm Hg) resting in the brachial artery. Elevated values index hypertension.

Serum Cholesterol

Serum cholesterol level (mg/dl) impacting atherosclerotic plaque formation inside coronary channels.

Chest Pain (Angina Type)

Categorized into Typical Angina, Atypical Angina, Non-Anginal pain, or Asymptomatic chest distress.

Resting ECG

Assesses abnormal ST-T waves or left ventricular hypertrophy indicating structural changes.

Exercise Angina & ST Slope

Measures physical workload-induced ischemic changes (angina) and the ST-segment slope at peak heart rate.
